# Evidence and authority

English | [简体中文](evidence-and-authority.zh-CN.md)

Evidence explains what was observed or produced. Authority determines what may be accepted or changed. They are related but never interchangeable.

An execution receipt should be exact-bound, normalized, and honest about uncertainty. Validation checks required facts; policy interprets permitted paths; an explicit human or owner gate controls high-impact transitions. When an external side effect, authority, or identity is ambiguous, the safe result is HOLD and reconciliation.
