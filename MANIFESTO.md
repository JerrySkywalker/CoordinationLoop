# Coordination Loop Manifesto

English | [简体中文](MANIFESTO.zh-CN.md)

Coordination Loop treats continuous agent-assisted development as a durable engineering program, not a sequence of chats.

## We center durable Goals

A Goal is a logical unit of progress with identity, scope, authority, evidence, and a place in a development-state graph. An agent can help execute it, but an agent is not the Goal and cannot become its durable identity.

## We separate authority from assertion

An execution report may be useful evidence, but it is not an authoritative transition on its own. Required receipts, validation, policy, and human/owner authority determine whether state can advance.

## We keep execution replaceable

Providers, machines, sessions, processes, and internal agent graphs are execution concerns. They may change without erasing development identity or silently expanding authority.

## We preserve explicit boundaries

Coordination Loop is Git/DevOps-native, evidence-driven, provider-independent, and machine-independent. It is not a generic agent runtime, workflow engine, model router, launcher, or all-powerful agent UI.

## We fail closed when truth is ambiguous

Ambiguous authority, identity, or external side-effect state is a reason to hold and reconcile—not to guess, retry, or declare success.
